Independent research

Research as a practice, not a position.

I maintain an independent, self-funded research practice in robust nonparametric statistics and spatial analysis, developed alongside my engineering work and in collaboration with researchers at Colombian universities. Current lines: robust multivariate estimators and high-dimensional outlier detection, nonparametric synthetic data generation via empirical copulas, and spatial statistical analysis applied to citizen security phenomena.

This work is not affiliated with, or funded by, any institution.
